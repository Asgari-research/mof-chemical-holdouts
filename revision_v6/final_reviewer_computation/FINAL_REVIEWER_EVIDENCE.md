| **R1.2** | What constitutes the geometry split? | **New code/metadata audit:** the executed pipeline reads external `geo-clusters.csv` with `filename` and `cluster_id`, renames the latter `geo_cluster`, drops missing labels and singleton groups, then uses five-fold `GroupKFold` with no label crossing train/test. | **R1.4** | Why do geometry, linker, and functional splits resemble random performance, and does geometric training support explain topology errors? | **New saved-data audit:** for enriched RF, CO₂ 0.15 bar, mean fold R² is random 0.7581, geometry 0.7393, functional 0.7487, linker 0.7510, versus topology 0.2674; these original split summaries use different eligible populations. Linker has 67.38% of eligible rows in size-2/3 groups. On the **existing V6** 177,411 paired MOFs, the **new** eight-feature, train-only standardized nearest-training-geometry diagnostic gives topology MAE 0.6426 mmol/g and 22.17% errors >1 mmol/g in the closest support quartile (44,353 MOFs), versus MAE 0.1990 in the farthest quartile (44,353). | Tiny held-out labels and retained nearby geometry are consistent with random-like performance, but do not prove its cause. The support association is descriptive and confounded by family: `fsc` has median distance 0.0576 and MAE 0.8995 (n=30,486), while `nbo` has distance 0.3277 and MAE 0.1619 (n=29,525). | “Geometry, functional, and linker group holdouts produce R² values close to random splitting in the saved enriched-RF folds, whereas topology holdout is much harder. Most retained geometry and linker groups are very small, so withholding a label need not remove nearby structures from training. In a topology-fold training-support check, large prediction errors persisted even for geometrically close held-out structures; this is an association, not evidence that proximity causally determines error.” | Results paragraph comparing split protocols; SI: group-size and nearest-training-support quartile tables. |
| **R2.1** | Do one-to-five random OOS predictions and their averaging change ranking? | **New five-fit, protocol-matched sensitivity:** all five 52,749-ID ShuffleSplit test memberships match the historical archive exactly, but the new 177,411-ID aggregate differs from the saved mean by 0.011105 mmol/g mean absolute prediction difference, so these are **not** exact historical prediction reconstructions. On the fixed 177,411-structure eligible cohort, 108,167 have one OOS prediction; five seeded one-prediction-per-structure samples have top-5% (k=8,871) Jaccard 0.9624–0.9654 with the new averaged list and true-reference recovery 0.6180–0.6194 versus 0.6203 for that average. | The comparison uses actual new fold predictions and one constant population, never splits historical averages into pseudo-repeats. | **R2.4** | Do additional available chemistry descriptors improve topology holdout? | **New complete-case paired RF control:** 256,328/263,744 MOFs (97.19%) have unique, nonmissing joins for six predeclared RAC chemistry proxies absent from the 20 original enriched-interpretable features; 7,416 are excluded. On identical five CO₂ 0.15-bar topology folds, fixed saved RF hyperparameters and train-only median imputation, RACs improve R²/MAE in folds 0–2 but worsen both in folds 3–4. Mean paired ΔR² is −0.2896, ΔMAE +0.09165 mmol/g, and Δ fold-local top-5% recovery +0.06173; `fsc` MAE improves 0.8865→0.7758 (n=45,498), while `pts` worsens 0.2550→0.6328 (n=37,211) and `rna` 0.8261→1.4516 (n=13,868). At CO2 0.015 bar, mean paired delta R2 is -0.3291, delta MAE +0.02365 mmol/g, and delta recovery +0.04379; `pts` worsens from 0.0358 to 0.1115 (n=37,211) and `rna` from 0.2162 to 0.3702 (n=13,868). | RACs encode chemistry proxies, not electrostatic charges. The 2.81% missing RAC coverage is nonuniform across databases/families, so these are paired complete-case results rather than a replacement for the original full-cohort benchmark; neither fold means nor isolated gains justify a uniform benefit claim. The adverse `pts` and `rna` effects recur at both CO2 pressures. | “We added six predeclared RAC chemistry proxies to the original 20-feature RF on the same covered structures and identical held-out topology folds, without retuning. Their effect was heterogeneous: three CO₂ 0.15-bar folds improved, but `pts` and `rna` deteriorated substantially; the same adverse families recur at the lower CO2 pressure. We therefore cannot claim that these available chemistry proxies reliably resolve topology transfer, and they do not constitute a direct electrostatics test.” | SI descriptor-control methods, per-fold metric table, and major-family error changes; Results/Discussion: qualify descriptor limitation rather than claim a remedy. |
